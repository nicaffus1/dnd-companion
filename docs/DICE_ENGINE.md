# Dice engine
Formula grammar: signed additive terms of standard dice (d4,d6,d8,d10,d12,d20,d100) and integer modifiers; maximum 50 rolled dice and total modifier ±1000. Critical damage doubles positive dice terms, not modifiers; maximum 25 base dice. Advantage/disadvantage requires one positive d20. Web Crypto rejection sampling avoids modulo bias. Results are persisted before display; animation is illustrative, not physics-based and does not encode actual face values. Numeric results are authoritative. Three.js meshes and reduced-motion/manual 2D fallback. Custom presets and history scoped to active campaign/character; grouped copy ordered oldest first. Character modifiers are manually entered until character sheet integration. No audio/vibration yet. d10/d100 use illustrative mesh. No external assets required.
