Inventory schema 6 stores per-character items, currency, optional capacity and attunement overrides. Starting equipment transfers on first finished build or first inventory save. Existing schema 5 inventories derive from saved equipment until first save. Imported unsupported mechanics remain editable. Weight is partial if unknown; coins 50/lb; nested weightless containers ignore contents. Equipped bonuses require attunement when marked. Weapon proficiency covers common class defaults, not all subclasses or special grants. Ammunition and consumables are manual quantity reductions; recharge is manual or fixed rest reset, not random dawn recharge. No automatic spell effects or ability-score-setting magic item effects. Inventory edits do not change old roll history.
